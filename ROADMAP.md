## Startup hypothesis

Initial customer: a 2–20-person software agency selling fixed-price projects and tracking work in GitHub issues. The paid product hypothesis is a hosted workspace that keeps agreed scope, change evidence, client approval, and payment status in one place. Pricing and market size are **unvalidated**. The first business milestone is a repeat user and a paid change order that would otherwise have been missed; it is not a slide-deck forecast.

## Scope discipline

The hackathon version demonstrates one complete request-to-payment journey. Email and Slack ingestion, escrow, payouts, autonomous prices, and formal e-signatures are later research topics only if customers ask for them.
